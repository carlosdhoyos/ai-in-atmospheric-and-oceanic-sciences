Planned contents

What this module will cover

Loss functions

Mean squared error, cross-entropy, losses for rare events and for whole maps; what each one rewards.

Backpropagation

The chain rule worked by hand through a two-layer network, then automatic differentiation as libraries do it.

Gradient descent and friends

Step size, mini-batches and stochastic gradients, momentum, Adam, learning-rate schedules and warm-up.

The landscape

Local minima, saddle points and plateaus; why random starts end in different places; why wide valleys generalize better.

Gradients that vanish or explode

Why deep stacks struggle, and how initialization, normalization and residual shortcuts fix it.

Regularization and stopping

Weight decay, dropout, early stopping and validation curves: training well without memorizing.

When the chain breaks

Kinks, hard decisions and sampling: surrogate gradients, straight-through and reparameterization tricks.

Black-box physics

Models with no derivatives: trial nudges, adjoint models as in 4D-Var data assimilation, differentiable physics (JAX).

Practice

Reading training curves, choosing a learning rate, and the usual failure signs, with atmospheric and ocean examples.
