      How to read this

      • Scope. These numbers describe the venues' papers in this catalog, a curated set of ML/AI work for weather, climate, ocean, water, energy and agriculture. They are not journal-wide statistics and are not the Clarivate Journal Impact Factor.
      • Impact index. For each paper, citations per year divided by the median citations per year of all catalog papers published in the same year. A venue's index is the median over its papers, so a single landmark paper cannot dominate it. Citation counts come from Semantic Scholar and Crossref.
      • Top-10% share. The share of a venue's papers whose relative citation rate is in the top 10% of the catalog.
      • Tiers (venues with 5+ papers): Leading index ≥ 1.5 and top-10% share ≥ 20%; Strong index ≥ 1.0; Steady index ≥ 0.6; Lower impact below that.
      • Caveats. Selection favoured well-cited and well-placed work, which flatters strong venues. Papers from the last year have had little time to be cited, and preprint-only work is listed separately.

      Metadata and citation counts were retrieved from Semantic Scholar, Crossref and arXiv on . Counts differ between indexes (Google Scholar is usually higher), so use them to compare papers, not as absolute numbers. Papers with both an arXiv preprint and a published version are listed once under the published venue.
